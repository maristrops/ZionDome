Technology

AI Rain Towers

Autonomous towers create large‑area rain & mist curtains to cool air, trap smoke particulates and stop fire spread along urban and natural perimeters.

  • Sensor fusion: wind, temperature, humidity, particulates
  • Local control + mesh networking for outage scenarios
  • Renewable / microgrid‑ready pumping systems

Aerogel Fire Barriers

Ultra‑light, high‑temp aerogel panels & coatings to harden critical infrastructure (buildings, transformers, pipelines, data sites) against heat and flame.

  • Extreme thermal protection; retrofit friendly
  • Supports city resilience & energy reliability

System Integration

Edge AI + cloud monitoring, secure APIs to municipal OPS and utility systems; optional satellite & LTE fallback.

  • KPIs: protected area, response time, ΔTemp, ΔPM2.5/PM10
  • Uptime under grid/comm outages

FAQ

How much water do towers use?

Depends on nozzle configuration and duty cycle; pilots are optimized for micro‑rain efficacy and water efficiency.

Can the system run during power outages?

Yes. Local control, backup power and mesh networking enable operation without grid or cellular, where permitted.

What about environmental impact?

We work with agencies to ensure water sourcing, drift control and local ecosystems are respected; aerogel materials are inert and long‑lasting.
